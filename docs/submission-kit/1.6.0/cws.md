## Recipe

1. When the GitHub Release `v1.6.0` exists, run the **Publish to
   stores** workflow (version `1.6.0`) and approve it in the
   `store-publish` environment. It uploads
   `brreg-snap-chrome-1.6.0.zip`, submits it for review, then
   tags `cws-submission-1.6.0`. CWS has no per-version release
   notes field.
2. By hand, and only if this release changed them: the store listing
   description and the privacy practices tab in the Developer
   Dashboard (sections below), per `docs/release.md` § What stays
   manual. The privacy policy URL serves `PRIVACY.md` on
   `main`, so it updates when `main` does.

## What changed (CHANGELOG § [1.6.0])

> ### Added
>
> - Økonomi shows how the company has done over the last few years:
>   «Utvikling 2022–2024», driftsinntekter and årsresultat for up to
>   three years in aligned columns, with the unit once beside each label,
>   and a plain sentence under them («Driftsinntekter ned 26 %, høyest i
>   2024.») that also says when the result turned from profit to loss or
>   back. A sentence never claims more than the years on screen show. A
>   latest filing without figures still shows the years before it.
>
> ### Changed
>
> - A new logo: a blue ribbon tied in an overhand knot replaces the gold
>   «B», on the toolbar and store icons and at the head of the popup, the
>   panel and the welcome page.
> - The store name and short description say what you get for the
>   company behind the site you are on, instead of the bare «brreg-snap»
>   and «Slå opp norske bedrifter i Brønnøysundregistrene med ett klikk.»
> - A danger verdict is a field framed in thin red on a faint tint: a red
>   «✕ Alvorlig» kicker, then the sentence in large type with only the
>   key word in red.
> - When brreg can't be reached to check a site, the answer comes first:
>   «Fikk ikke svar fra Brønnøysundregistrene» with «Prøv igjen», then
>   the search. The heading «Fikk ikke sjekket …» that said the same is
>   gone.
> - A sole proprietorship names its owner: «Innehaver» in the popup and
>   first under Ledelse in the panel.
> - One way of saying things: every date reads «26. aug. 2026», every
>   search field says «Navn eller org.nr», an org.nr that finds nothing
>   reads «Fant ikke noe selskap med org.nr …», and missing accounts are
>   explained in the register's words instead of «brreg sitt åpne API».
> - Less repetition: the popup's search view drops the line under its
>   heading that repeated the placeholder, the picker's «Eller søk selv»
>   field starts empty instead of holding the site's name, and on a tab
>   with no address the popup shows the logo alone over «Ingen nettside
>   å slå opp».
> - An org.nr that belongs to a branch: the «Avdeling: …» line mutes the
>   part of the branch's name it shares with the parent, as Enheter does.
> - The popup shows the loading outline under the site's name as soon as
>   it opens, instead of a blank strip while it finds the company.
> - The panel's back bar underlines on hover, «Kopier sammendrag» sits
>   at the right edge with or without registry flags before it, and the
>   equity bar's empty part is a hairline grey, not the loading grey.
> - [chrome] Search fields no longer show Chrome's built-in clear «×»,
>   the one control not drawn in the extension's own colours.
>
> ### Fixed
>
> - With auto-sync off, a lookup in the popup could set or clear the
>   toolbar badge of the tab the panel followed before. The badge now
>   marks only the tab whose company is on screen.
> - The focus ring inside a danger verdict is clearly visible; screen
>   readers hear the equity bar's legend; «Endret nylig» keeps its
>   neutral mark next to a merknad; the panel's back navigation no longer
>   leaves an empty landmark.
> - A site whose lookup partly failed could keep a cached direct answer
>   where a complete lookup shows a picker.
> - Short subsidiary keywords no longer match inside longer words (IT in
>   SPIRIT, CARE in SKINCARE), and a site with æ, ø or å in its domain
>   ties to the company that registered it.
> - A failed roles fetch showed as a row that read like data («Roller:
>   Kunne ikke hentes»); Ledelse now says it in a sentence, as Personer
>   does: «Kunne ikke hente roller. Prøv igjen senere.»
> - The panel's loading outline has a Kobling row only when the answer
>   will, so the rows no longer jump when it fills in. A short company
>   (a deleted one's Registrering) no longer scrolls into blank space
>   before you use its tabs.
> - Choosing a company from the picker, a search or the recent list
>   with the keyboard puts focus on the result, and a failed lookup puts
>   it on the error; «Oppdater» in the popup keeps focus on itself.
> - At narrow panel widths a «·» no longer starts a line, a postcode
>   stays with its place, a legal form («AS») or a count is no longer
>   left alone on a line, and «Rapporter feil treff» stays at the right.
>   In the picker, every row's tag sits in the same place.
> - An amount too small for the Økonomi trend's unit («<0,1») reads
>   exactly on hover and to screen readers («12 345 kr»), not rounded.

## Store listing description (nb, plain text)

```
Brønnøysundregistrene, rett fra nettsiden du er på. Ett klikk slår opp bedriften bak siden: organisasjonsnummer, status og det viktigste fra registeret.

• Se hvordan siden henger sammen med bedriften: registrert hjemmeside, et org.nr siden selv oppgir, eller bare et navnetreff.
• Sidepanelet går dypere: roller, adresser, næring og formål, konsern og underenheter, nøkkeltall som omsetning og resultat over flere år der de åpne dataene har dem, og lenker til årsregnskap og kunngjøringer.
• Varsler i registeret, som konkurs, avvikling eller manglende regnskap, vises tydelig. Ingen varsler er likevel ingen garanti.
• Søk på navn eller org.nr, slå opp markert tekst med høyreklikk, eller bruk en hurtigtast.
• Velg en annen bedrift ved feil treff, og kopier org.nr eller et sammendrag.

Med «Auto-oppdater» følger et åpent sidepanel fanen du ser på. Du får først en forklaring, deretter spør nettleseren om tilgang til faner. Slår du det av, gis tilgangen tilbake.

Personvern: Oppslag sendes bare til data.brreg.no, aldri til utvikleren. Utvidelsen sender nettstedets registrerbare domene, et organisasjonsnummer eller søketeksten du selv skriver eller velger å slå opp. Den har ingen content scripts og leser ikke nettsideinnhold automatisk. Ingen analytics, trackere eller telemetri. Velkomstsiden gjør ingen nettverkskall. «Rapporter feil treff» åpner e-postprogrammet ditt; du velger selv om meldingen sendes.

brreg-snap er et uavhengig prosjekt, ikke tilknyttet Brønnøysundregistrene, og bruker åpne data fra data.brreg.no.

Kildekode under MIT-lisens: https://github.com/ikkeseb/brreg-snap
```

## Store listing description (en, plain text)

```
Norway's company register, right from the site you are on. One click looks up the company behind the page in the Brønnøysund Register Centre and shows its organisation number, its status and the essentials from the register.

• See how the site connects to the company: its registered website, an organisation number stated on the site, or only a name match.
• The side panel goes deeper: roles, addresses, industry and purpose, group structure and sub-units, key figures such as revenue and profit over several years where the open data has them, and links to annual accounts and announcements.
• Registry warnings stand out, such as bankruptcy, liquidation or overdue accounts. A clean record is still no guarantee.
• Search by name or organisation number, look up selected text from the right-click menu, or use a keyboard shortcut.
• Pick another company after a wrong match and copy the organisation number or a summary.

With "Auto-oppdater" on, an open side panel follows your active tab. A short explanation comes first, then the browser asks for tab access. Turning it off gives the permission back.

Privacy: Lookups go only to data.brreg.no, never to the developer. The extension sends the site's registrable domain, an organisation number or search text you type or choose to look up. It has no content scripts and does not read page content automatically. No analytics, trackers or telemetry. The welcome page makes no network requests. "Rapporter feil treff" opens your email app; you decide whether to send the message.

brreg-snap is an independent project, not affiliated with the Brønnøysund Register Centre, and uses open data from data.brreg.no.

Source code under the MIT licence: https://github.com/ikkeseb/brreg-snap
```

## Privacy practices tab

1. **Single purpose** (required free text):
   > Look up Norwegian companies in the Brønnøysund Register Centre
   > (Brreg) directly from the browser: status, roles, parent unit and
   > sub-units, and key financial figures, sourced live from the
   > official Brreg open-data API.

2. **Remote code:** select **"No, I am not using remote code."**
   (MV3 + CSP `default-src 'self'` = none.)

3. **Data usage — what is collected:** tick **Web history** and
   nothing else. The extension sends the registrable domain of the
   site the user looks up (dnb.no for nettbank.dnb.no) to
   data.brreg.no (the CWS User Data FAQ counts "the domains or URLs
   the browser interacts with" as web browsing activity). Lookups run
   when the user asks for one; "Auto-oppdater" runs only after the user
   confirms an in-panel disclosure and grants `tabs` (see the `tabs`
   justification below). Leave unchecked: personally identifiable
   information, health, financial and payment, authentication,
   personal communications, location, user activity, website content.
   If the form asks what the data is used for: core functionality
   only.

4. **Data usage — certifications:** tick all three; all true here.
   The transfer to data.brreg.no is the single purpose itself, which
   the Limited Use policy allows. The listing shows them as: not sold
   to third parties outside the approved use cases; not used or
   transferred for purposes unrelated to the item's core
   functionality; not used or transferred to determine
   creditworthiness or for lending.

5. **Limited Use statement:** CWS requires an affirmative Limited Use
   statement on the website or privacy policy. It lives in
   `PRIVACY.md` § Store declarations:
   > brreg-snap's use of information received from Chrome extension
   > APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including
   > the Limited Use requirements.

6. **Permission justifications** (a free-text box per declared
   permission — reviewers read these; unjustified = rejection):

   | Permission | Justification |
   |-----------|---------------|
   | `activeTab` | Reads the URL and title of the active tab only after the user clicks the toolbar icon or a context-menu item, or uses a keyboard shortcut, to find the company behind that site in Brreg. Only an organisation number found in the URL or title, or else the site's registrable domain (dnb.no for nettbank.dnb.no) and a name label derived from it, is sent to data.brreg.no for that lookup; the page content is never read. |
   | `storage` | Caches Brreg lookup results and the user's picker choice per site (24 h, in session storage, cleared when the browser closes), keeps the 5 most recent companies, and stores the on/off setting for "Auto-oppdater". Local only; nothing is synced. |
   | `contextMenus` | Adds right-click items to look up the current page or selected text. Text is sent to data.brreg.no only when the user chooses that lookup, limited to 100 characters; if it contains exactly one valid organisation number, only that number is sent. |
   | `sidePanel` | Shows the detailed company view (roles, parent unit, sub-units, key figures) in Chrome's side panel alongside the page. |
   | host `https://data.brreg.no/*` | The extension's sole network endpoint: all company data is fetched read-only from the official Brønnøysund open-data API. No other hosts are contacted; there are no content scripts. |
   | `tabs` *(optional)* | Requested at runtime only when the user turns on "Auto-oppdater" in the side panel, so an open panel can look up the page in front every time the user switches tabs or opens a new page. Turning it on first shows an in-panel disclosure: pages are looked up while a brreg-snap panel is open, the domain goes to data.brreg.no, nothing goes to the developer. Its "Slå på" button calls permissions.request; turning the setting off calls permissions.remove. Used only while a side panel is open. Not requested at install time. |
